Movie Recommendation System

Pick a movie you like and get a ranked list of similar ones. The notebook reads a movies CSV, turns each movie's text into numbers, and compares them.

Open the Colab notebook

How it works

  1. Load the dataThe movies CSV is downloaded from Google Drive into the notebook.
  2. Vectorize with TF-IDFEach movie's text (genres, keywords, overview) becomes a weighted word vector. Rare words count more than common ones.
  3. Compare with cosine similarityMovies whose vectors point the same way score close to 1.
  4. RecommendSort by score and return the top matches.
